# Start Fresh With a Monthly Budget

Budgeting doesn't have to be complicated. With MyMI Wallet, you can create your first monthly budget in minutes. Begin by categorizing your income and expenses, then track your progress throughout the month. Our intuitive dashboards provide clear insights so you can adjust as needed and meet your financial goals.

### Tips for Beginners
- Review last month's spending to set realistic targets.
- Use alerts to avoid overspending.
- Celebrate small wins to stay motivated.
