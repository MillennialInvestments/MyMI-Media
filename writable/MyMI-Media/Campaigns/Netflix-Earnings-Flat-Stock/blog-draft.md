# Netflix’s Earnings Surge—But the Stock Stalls

Netflix reported strong numbers across the board: new subscribers, improved margins, and healthy cash flow. So why didn’t the stock respond? Sometimes, markets anticipate positive results ahead of time, or investors focus on future guidance instead of past performance.

If the company’s outlook doesn’t exceed expectations, even good earnings can leave the stock stuck in neutral. MyMI Wallet lets you monitor guidance, analyst upgrades, and volume trends so you know when sentiment shifts.
