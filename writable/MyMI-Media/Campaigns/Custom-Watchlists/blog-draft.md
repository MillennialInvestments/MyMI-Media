# The Power of Custom Watchlists

Successful investing starts with focusing on the right assets. MyMI Wallet lets you build multiple watchlists, each tailored to a specific strategy or market sector. In this post we discuss tips for organizing your lists, setting price alerts, and leveraging analytics to spot trends before they take off.

## Best Practices
- Create separate lists for long-term holds vs short-term trades.
- Use color coding to highlight priority assets.
- Review and adjust your lists regularly.
