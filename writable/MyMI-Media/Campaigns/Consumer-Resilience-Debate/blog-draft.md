# The Consumer Resilience Debate

While corporate earnings calls tout unstoppable consumer spending, many families are juggling rising costs and lower savings. Survey data suggests confidence is slipping even as companies maintain upbeat forecasts.

MyMI Wallet helps bridge the gap between corporate optimism and your personal finances. By tracking your spending across categories, you can see if you're keeping pace or falling behind.

## Consider This
1. Higher credit card balances signal financial stress.
2. Wage growth may not keep up with inflation for all workers.
3. Real-world budgets reveal more than macro statistics.

Is corporate optimism justified? Dive into the data and decide for yourself.
