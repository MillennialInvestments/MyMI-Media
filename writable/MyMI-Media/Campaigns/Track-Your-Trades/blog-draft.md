# Staying Organized with the MyMI Trade Tracker

Active trading requires discipline and detailed record keeping. The MyMI Trade Tracker consolidates your trades into one easy dashboard, highlighting win rates, average returns, and more. This blog explores how real-time alerts can help you execute strategies swiftly and review performance with confidence.

### Features
- Automatic trade logging and categorization.
- Real-time notifications on critical price levels.
- Exportable reports for tax season.

With everything in one place, you can focus on making profitable decisions.
