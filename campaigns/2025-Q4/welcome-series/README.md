# MyMI Wallet — Q4 2025 Welcome Series (100-Day Campaign)

This pack contains platform-ready posts, video/voiceover scripts, slide outlines, holiday variants, and a 100-day calendar mapping files to dates/platforms.

**Core theme:** _Welcome to MyMI Wallet — Intro to all core services._  
**Period:** Sep 22, 2025 → Dec 31, 2025 (100 days)  
**Posting logic:** Rotates services + platforms, injects holiday-themed variants on the exact date.

## Structure
- `posts/facebook_linkedin/` — Long-form platform copy (no external tracking links embedded).
- `posts/x_twitter/` — Short posts with hashtag clusters.
- `posts/stocktwits/` — Linkless insights focused on value-add around tickers.
- `posts/discord/` — Announcements compatible with own + 3rd-party servers; avoid links when disallowed.
- `video_scripts/shorts/` — 30–40s scripts for TikTok/YouTube/IG Reels.
- `voiceovers/` — Voiceover scripts matching video themes.
- `slides_outlines/` — 5-slide vertical storyboards for quick rendering.
- `holidays/` — Themed variants for Halloween, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Christmas, New Year’s Eve.
- `calendar/100_day_schedule.csv` — Day-by-day mapping (service, platform, file path).

## Services covered
Personal Budgeting (Free), Investment Portfolio Management, Automated Account Integration, MyMI Trade Alerts & Tracker (Free + Premium), MyMI Gold + Membership Discounts (60% off annual), MyMI Solana Exchange / Coin Swap, MyMI Asset Creator & Projects (Beta/Waitlist), Retirement Planner (Free + Premium), Real-Time Data & Custom Alerts, Community & Referral Program

## How to use
1. Open `calendar/100_day_schedule.csv` to see today’s item.
2. Open the referenced file, paste into your scheduler (Buffer/Zapier/Discord).  
3. For videos: use `video_scripts/shorts/*.md` and pair with `voiceovers/*.txt` + `slides_outlines/*.md`.

## Notes
- StockTwits/3rd-party Discord: **no external links**; position MyMI as a helpful tool inside the conversation.
- Feel free to adjust hashtags per news cycle.
- Build regional spinoffs by altering CTAs/lines in each file.
